Frequently Asked Questions (Water Softener Salt Setting Chart)

How many bags of salt does the Water Softener Salt Setting Chart use per month?

With 4 people and 17.0 compensated GPG (5,100 grains/day), regenerating every 7 days at 12.0 lbs per cycle uses approximately 51 lbs of salt per month—or about 15 standard 40-lb bags per year.

Should I use 8% or 10% crosslink resin for the Water Softener Salt Setting Chart?

If you are on chlorinated city water, always choose 10% crosslink cation resin paired with a carbon pre-filter. Chlorine oxidizes standard 8% resin into mush within 4–6 years, causing severe water pressure drops.

Is softened water from the Water Softener Salt Setting Chart safe to drink?

Softened water is safe for most adults, but at 15 GPG hardness the ion-exchange process adds roughly 112 mg of sodium per liter of water. Water treatment engineers strongly recommend installing an under-sink Reverse Osmosis (RO) faucet at the kitchen sink for drinking and cooking.
